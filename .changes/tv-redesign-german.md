The TV app has a new look and speaks German. Home shows three large tiles for Live TV, Movies and Series with a clock, a top bar holds search, favorites, sources and settings, Live TV lists categories with country flags next to channel cards with HD/EPG badges and a "Now playing" preview, and the player shows a Live badge, the current programme and previous/next channel buttons. The language can be switched between German and English in Settings → Appearance.
