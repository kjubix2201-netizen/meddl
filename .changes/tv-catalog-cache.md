The channel, film and series lists of an Xtream source are kept on the
device and downloaded again only every 24 hours, so meddl starts at once
instead of loading everything on every start. If the provider does not
answer, the last lists are used. Settings → Library & sync sets the interval
(every start, 6, 24 or 72 hours) and has "Reload lists now".
